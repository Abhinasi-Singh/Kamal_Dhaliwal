Everything You Need to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Rent to Own is a powerful tool when structured properly. Here are straightforward answers to common buyer questions.

How does Rent to Own work in Ontario? +

Rent to Own pairs a standard residential lease with an Option to Purchase agreement. You live in the home, pay monthly rent (part of which is set aside as equity credits), and have the exclusive right to purchase the home at a pre-agreed price at the end of the term.

What initial option fee or deposit is required? +

Initial option consideration fees are typically significantly lower than a full 20% down payment (usually starting around 3–5%), and 100% of this upfront payment goes directly toward your final home purchase price.

What if home prices rise during my lease period? +

Your purchase price is locked in upfront when you sign your agreement. If GTA market values increase during your lease, the gain in equity belongs entirely to you upon purchase!

Who is eligible for the Rent to Own program? +

Buyers with steady household income who require time to build up down payment savings, establish self-employment history, or improve credit scores before obtaining conventional bank mortgage approval.

Can I choose any home for sale in the GTA? +

Yes! Unlike traditional rental properties, Rent-to-Own allows you to select qualifying residential single-family homes, townhouses, or condos directly on the open MLS market.
