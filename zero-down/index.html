Everything You Need to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Zero down payment options are powerful tools when structured properly. Here are straightforward answers to common buyer questions.

How does a zero down payment home purchase work in Ontario? +

Zero down payment options utilize approved lender structures, flex-down mortgage products, or financial incentives where the traditional down payment requirement is covered through specialized financing, borrowed down payments, or lender credits. We help you evaluate qualifying programs tailored to your financial profile.

Does zero down mean there are no out-of-pocket costs at all? +

Not necessarily. While upfront down payment barriers are eliminated or minimized, buyers should budget for standard closing expenses such as lawyer fees, home inspection costs, land transfer tax (rebates apply for eligible first-time buyers), and initial earnest deposits. We provide a complete line-by-line breakdown upfront.

What credit score or income is required to qualify? +

Qualification requires a steady, verifiable household income and a satisfactory credit rating (typically 650+ for conventional flex-down and zero-down options). During your confidential review, we evaluate your income and credit profile to establish what programs match your position.

Can I buy any residential home in the GTA with zero down? +

Eligible properties must meet standard mortgage insurer guidelines (Sagen, Canada Guaranty, or CMHC) and lender criteria. Most owner-occupied single-family homes, townhouses, and residential condos across Toronto, Brampton, Mississauga, and York Region qualify.

Is this program suitable for first-time home buyers? +

Yes! First-time buyers can combine zero-down financing options with government incentives like Ontario Land Transfer Tax rebates (up to $4,000), First Home Savings Accounts (FHSA), and the Home Buyers' Plan (HBP) for maximum financial advantage.
