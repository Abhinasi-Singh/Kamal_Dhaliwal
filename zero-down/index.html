Good questions, straightforward answers

Everything you need to know about zero down.

Every buyer's situation is unique. Here are the most common questions GTA buyers ask about zero down purchasing.

How does a zero down payment home purchase work in Ontario? +

Zero down payment options utilize approved lender structures, flex-down programs, or financial incentives where the traditional down payment requirement is covered through specialized financing, borrowed down payments, or lender credits. We help you explore options suited to your financial situation.

Does zero down mean there are no out-of-pocket costs at all? +

Not necessarily. While down payment barriers are reduced or minimized, buyers should account for standard closing costs such as legal fees, home inspections, land transfer taxes (rebates may apply for first-time buyers), and initial earnest deposits. We provide a full cost breakdown upfront.

What credit score or income is required to qualify? +

Qualification requires a steady, verifiable household income and a satisfactory credit rating (typically 650+ for conventional zero/flex-down programs). During your initial review, we evaluate your income and credit profile to establish what programs you may qualify for.

Can I buy any residential home in the GTA with zero down? +

Eligible properties must meet standard mortgage insurer guidelines (such as Sagen, Canada Guaranty, or CMHC) and lender criteria. Most owner-occupied single-family homes, townhouses, and condos across the GTA qualify.

Is this program suitable for first-time home buyers? +

Yes! First-time buyers can combine zero-down financing options with government incentives like Ontario Land Transfer Tax rebates, the Home Buyers' Plan (HBP), and First Home Savings Accounts (FHSA) for maximum financial advantage.
